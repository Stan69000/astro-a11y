Learning mode: issues are grouped by topic and explained in plain words. Start with the critical ones, then try each problem yourself: it is the best way to understand it.

What to fix

CRITICAL

1. Form control is missing a label

RGAA 11.1 — Chaque champ de formulaire a-t-il une étiquette ?

Why
Form fields without labels are difficult or impossible to understand with assistive technologies.
Who is affected
  • Blind people using a screen reader
  • People using voice control
  • People with attention or cognitive disorders
See it for yourself
Click on the text next to the field: if the cursor does not move into the field, the label is not linked.
Fix
Associate a visible or programmatic label with each form control.
Before
<input id="email" type="email">
After
<label for="email">Email</label><input id="email" type="email">
Where : 1 page(s), 1 element(s)
  • /contact/ — input
CRITICAL

2. Button has no accessible name

RGAA 11.9 — Dans chaque formulaire, l’intitulé de chaque bouton est-il pertinent (hors cas particuliers) ?

Why
Assistive technologies cannot announce the purpose of the button.
Who is affected
  • Blind people using a screen reader
  • People using voice control
See it for yourself
Turn on the screen reader of your device (VoiceOver: Cmd+F5 on Mac; NVDA, free, on Windows) and listen to the element.
Fix
Add visible text, aria-label, or an accessible name linked to the button.
Before
<button><svg aria-hidden="true"></svg></button>
After
<button aria-label="Close dialog"><svg aria-hidden="true"></svg></button>
Where : 1 page(s), 1 element(s)
  • / — button
MAJOR

3. Document has no title

RGAA 8.5 — Chaque page web a-t-elle un titre de page ?

Why
A meaningful page title helps users understand where they are.
Who is affected
  • Blind people using a screen reader
  • People with attention or cognitive disorders
See it for yourself
Open several tabs: can you tell which tab is this page?
Fix
Ensure each page has a descriptive title element.
Before
<title></title>
After
<title>Contact – Example Company</title>
Where : 1 page(s), 1 element(s)
  • /contact/ — html

4. HTML element has no lang attribute

RGAA 8.3 — Dans chaque page web, la langue par défaut est-elle présente ?

Why
Assistive technologies need the page language to pronounce content correctly.
Who is affected
  • Blind people using a screen reader
See it for yourself
Listen to the page with a screen reader: is the French read with an English accent?
Fix
Set the lang attribute on the root html element.
Before
<html>
After
<html lang="fr">

5. Insufficient color contrast

RGAA 3.2 — Dans chaque page web, le contraste entre la couleur du texte et la couleur de son arrière-plan est-il suffisamment élevé (hors cas particuliers) ?

Why
Low contrast makes text difficult to read for many users, including people with low vision.
Who is affected
  • People with low vision
  • Everyone, on a phone or in bright sunlight
See it for yourself
Read the page on your phone outdoors, or lower the screen brightness.
Fix
Increase the contrast ratio between foreground and background colors.
Before
.muted { color: #777; background: #888; }
After
.muted { color: #1f2937; background: #ffffff; }
Where : 1 page(s), 1 element(s)
  • / — p

6. Iframe has no title

RGAA 2.1 — Chaque cadre a-t-il un titre de cadre ?

Why
Users need a clear title to understand embedded content.
Who is affected
  • Blind people using a screen reader
See it for yourself
Turn on the screen reader of your device (VoiceOver: Cmd+F5 on Mac; NVDA, free, on Windows) and listen to the element.
Fix
Add a concise and meaningful title attribute to the iframe.
Before
<iframe src="https://example.com/embed"></iframe>
After
<iframe src="https://example.com/embed" title="Video player"></iframe>

  • / — iframe
MAJOR

7. Alternative text missing on image

RGAA 1.1 — Chaque image porteuse d’information a-t-elle une alternative textuelle ?

Why
Screen reader users cannot understand the purpose of the image.
Who is affected
  • Blind people using a screen reader
See it for yourself
Disable images in your browser (or read the page with a screen reader): is the information still there?
Fix
Add a meaningful alt attribute, or alt="" for decorative images.
Before
<img src="/team.jpg">
After
<img src="/team.jpg" alt="Team gathered in the Lyon office">
Where : 1 page(s), 1 element(s)
  • / — img
